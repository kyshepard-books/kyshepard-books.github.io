The 10-Step Japan PR Self-Audit (2027 Edition)

Run these checks before you apply — with documents, not memory. Any "not sure" is a finding to fix first.

  1. Eligibility: 10 continuous years (5+ on work/residence status), or points/spouse route. Points held for the whole period, with proof.
  2. Longest period of stay: if you hold 3 years, check your dates against April 1, 2027.
  3. Taxes: 5 years of resident tax certificates; receipts for any self-paid period.
  4. Pension & health insurance: record checked month by month; no gaps around job changes; spouse too.
  5. Household income: above the average for a Japanese household of your size, and stable.
  6. Future pension: projected amount considered; savings/investments to cover a shortfall.
  7. Reports: every job change and address change reported within 14 days; missed ones filed now.
  8. Time abroad: no single trip of 6+ months; total under 2.5 years in 10; employer proof for business trips.
  9. 2027 items: Japanese at B1 unless exempt; children in compulsory schooling; Guidebook on Living and Working read.
  10. Stability & documents: nothing big likely to change during the 12-18 month review; guarantor secured; current official document list used.

This is the short version. The refusal stories behind each step, and what to do when an answer is "no," are in Why Japan Says No (2027 Edition) on Amazon.
