About Ky Shepard

Ky Shepard is an IT consultant based in Japan who has worked at an Indian IT services company and at global enterprise software companies in Japan. He writes practical, plain-English guides for people building a career and a life in Japan, based on official rules and real cases from the foreign community.

He writes as a shepherd who navigates by the stars: the stars are the reliable facts and official rules, the compass you can steer by in the dark, and the one bright North Star is your destination — the job, the visa, the life in Japan you are aiming for.
