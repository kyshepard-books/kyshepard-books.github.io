About Ky Shepard

Ky Shepard is the guide readers turn to when the official answer is "it depends."

An IT consultant who has built his career across India, Japan and global enterprise technology, Shepard has spent years working inside the systems that quietly decide people's futures — the applications, the points tables, the fine print. What he learned, he writes down: clear, rigorously sourced guides that turn opaque rules into decisions you can act on.

His books are written for one reader: the person standing where he once stood, facing a system that refuses to explain itself. No hype, no guesswork — primary sources, real cases, and a map you can verify yourself.

The name is a promise. A shepherd doesn't carry you. He shows you the way — and the stars, the fixed facts, are how you navigate.

Navigate by the stars.
