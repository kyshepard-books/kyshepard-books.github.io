Privacy Policy

Operator: Ky Shepard
Last updated: 28 November 2026

What this app is

Ky Shepard Threads Manager is a private operations tool used by the author Ky Shepard to run a single Threads account: @kyshepard. It connects to that account through Meta's official Threads API. It is not a public service, it is not offered to other users, and no one else can sign up for or connect an account to it.

What data the app accesses

Through the Threads API, and only with the account owner's authorization, the app accesses:

  1. The @kyshepard account's own data — its profile information, its posts, replies to its posts, posts that mention it, and performance statistics (such as views, likes, replies, and link clicks) for its own posts and profile.
  2. Public posts by other Threads users — found through the API's keyword search. These are posts that are already public on Threads. The app reads a post's text, timestamp, permalink, and author username so the account owner can decide whether to reply to it.

The app does not access private accounts, direct messages, email addresses, phone numbers, or any data from people who have not made it public on Threads.

How the data is used

Data accessed by the app is used for one purpose only: operating the @kyshepard account itself — drafting and publishing its posts, replying to public posts and to people who engage with the account, and reviewing how the account's own posts perform.

The app does not:

  • sell data to anyone;
  • share data with advertisers or data brokers;
  • use data for advertising targeting;
  • build profiles of other Threads users;
  • use other users' public posts for anything beyond reading them and, where relevant, replying to them on Threads.

What is stored, and for how long

  • Access token: the app stores one access token for the @kyshepard account so it can call the API. It is kept in secure credential storage, is never published, and is refreshed or revoked when it expires or when access is removed.
  • Operating logs: minimal logs of the account's own activity (for example, which posts the account published and which posts it replied to, with timestamps) are kept so the operator can review and improve the account's content. Logs are kept only as long as they are useful for that purpose.
  • Other users' public posts: search results are processed at the time they are viewed. The app does not maintain a database of other users' posts. If a post is replied to, a reference to that post (its permalink) may appear in the operating log described above.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of data associated with you at any time:

  • If you are the account owner: revoke the app's access at any time in Threads under Settings → Account → Website permissions, and/or email kyshepard.books@gmail.com. On revocation or request, the stored access token and operating logs for the account are deleted.
  • If you are another Threads user: the app does not store your posts in a database. If a reference to one of your public posts (a permalink) appears in an operating log because @kyshepard replied to it, email kyshepard.books@gmail.com with a link to the post and the reference will be removed.

Deletion requests sent by email will be actioned promptly and confirmed by reply email.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
